Apartment buildings within a 15-minute walk of the Louisiana–Pearl and I-25 & Broadway RTD light rail stations, South Denver.

    How walk times are measured

    Walk times use real street and path distances from the OpenStreetMap foot router at 3 mph (80 m per minute), so 15 minutes is about 1,200 m (0.75 mi). The shaded areas are those 15-minute walking zones, not straight-line circles. Building locations come from the US Census geocoder, so a pin may sit at the address point rather than the front door.
